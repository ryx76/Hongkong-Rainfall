# Process

## Tools
I used ChatGPT to help understand the assignment, find an official rainfall dataset, and develop the Python code. Most of plot.py was written by ChatGPT. It also helped explain terminal errors and generated previews for me to review. 

My main contribution was deciding how the data should be presented and reviewing the previews. I asked ChatGPT to revise the code based on my feedback rather than writing most of the plotting code myself.

## Kept
I kept the suggestion to use the Hong Kong Observatory's daily rainfall data for 2025. It covers a complete year and lets me compare rainfall across different parts of the year. I also kept the bar chart and the red highlight for the highest value, since they make differences in rainfall easy to see.

After reviewing the first daily chart, I asked to group the records into seven-day periods and show the average daily rainfall for each period. The calculation divides each period's total rainfall by its actual number of days, including the final period, which contains only December 31. I asked for values above every bar and an orange line connecting the tops of the bars.

In a later preview, I noticed that week numbers alone did not make the months clear. I asked for a month timeline below the week labels, then asked to move it closer to them. These revisions produced the final layout.

## Rejected
I rejected the original daily layout because 365 narrow bars made the chart crowded and difficult to read. Weekly averages gave me a clearer overview, although they hide differences between individual days.

I also rejected the text and arrow pointing to the highest value. With a red bar and a number above it, the extra annotation felt unnecessary. I removed it while keeping the red highlight.
